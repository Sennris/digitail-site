Our Ethos Tā Mātou Kaupapa 🐾

> Community First > Hapori Tuatahi

We believe games are meant to be shared. From open dev logs to Discord playtests, we want our players involved from day one. No secrets, just transparent game development. E whakapono ana mātou me tuari ngā kēmu. Mai i ngā rātaka tuwhera ki ngā whakamātau Discord, e hiahia ana mātou kia uru mai ā mātou kaitākaro. Kāore he kōrero muna, he whanaketanga kēmu mārama noa iho.

> Beautiful Disasters > Ngā Aituā Ātaahua

Perfection is boring. We lean into the chaotic nature of coding to find mechanics that feel unique. If a glitch is fun, we make it a feature. He hōhā te tino tika. Ka whirinaki mātou ki te āhua rerekē o te waehere kia kitea ai ngā āhuatanga motuhake. Ki te ngahau tētahi hapa, ka whakahuatia e mātou hei āhuatanga kēmu.
