Privacy Tūmataiti

Last updated: August 2026 Whakahōutanga whakamutunga: Ākuhata 2026

Digi Tail Studios is a small independent game studio in Ōtautahi Christchurch. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get it removed. We are covered by the Privacy Act 2020. He tapuhi kēmu motuhake iti a Digi Tail Studios i Ōtautahi. Ka whakamārama tēnei whārangi he aha ā mātou e kohi ai, he aha ai, me pēhea te tango. E raro mātou i te Ture Tūmataiti 2020.

If you join the mailing list Mēnā ka uru koe ki te rārangi īmēra

We keep your email address, the name you gave us if you gave one, and the date you signed up. That is all. We do not ask for anything else and we do not store your location or your IP address. Ka puritia e mātou tō wāhitau īmēra, tō ingoa mēnā i hoatu koe, me te rā i uru mai ai koe. Koia anake. Kāore mātou e pātai atu ki tētahi atu mea, kāore hoki e pupuri i tō wāhi, i tō wāhitau IP rānei.

We use it to send you studio updates, devlogs and playtest invitations. Nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it, or hand it to anyone for advertising. Ka whakamahia hei tuku whakahōu tapuhi, rātaka whanaketanga, me ngā tono whakamātau. Kāore atu. Kāore mātou e hoko, e reti, e hoatu rānei ki tētahi atu mō te pānui whakatairanga.

Who else sees it Ko wai anō ka kite

Two companies help us run this, and only these two: E rua ngā kamupene hei āwhina i a mātou, ko ēnei anake:

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and its database, runs the "are you a person" check on our forms, and counts page views for us. That check sees your IP address, as any website host does. We do not keep it. Cloudflare e whakahaere ana i tēnei pae me tōna pātengi raraunga, me te arowhai "he tangata koe". Ka kite taua arowhai i tō wāhitau IP, pērā i ngā kaiwhakahaere paetukutuku katoa. Kāore mātou e pupuri.
  • Buttondown sends the emails. Your address is stored with them as well as with us. Buttondown e tuku ana i ngā īmēra. Ka puritia tō wāhitau ki a rātou, ki a mātou hoki.

Getting off the list Te wehe atu i te rārangi

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can also use our unsubscribe page at any time. If you want your details deleted entirely rather than just marked as unsubscribed, email us and we will do it. Kei ia īmēra tētahi hono wehe atu, ā, ka taea hoki te whakamahi i tā mātou whārangi wehe atu i ngā wā katoa. Mēnā e hiahia ana koe kia mukua ō taipitopito, īmēra mai ka mahia e mātou.

You have the right to ask what we hold about you and to have it corrected. Ask us at hello@digitailstudios.com. Kei a koe te mana ki te pātai he aha ā mātou e pupuri ana mōu, me te whakatika. Pātai mai ki hello@digitailstudios.com.

Visitor numbers

We count page views so we can tell which parts of the site people actually use. That is done with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie, does not fingerprint your device, and cannot follow you to any other website.

There is no profile of you held anywhere, because there is nothing to build one from. We see totals, not people.

If you send us fan art

The fan art form asks for the name you want to be credited by, a link to your art, and optionally a link to you and an email address. We keep what you send, along with a copy of the exact permission wording you agreed to and the date you agreed to it, so we can always answer the question of what you said yes to.

Your email address is only ever used to talk to you about your art. It is never added to the newsletter, never shared, and never sold. Leaving it blank is fine.

Only the name you chose, your title, your link and the artwork itself are ever shown publicly. If you would like your art taken down or your credit changed, email us and we will do it.

Cookies Pihikete

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The only cookie it ever sets is the one that keeps studio staff logged in to the admin area, and you will never see it. Kāore tēnei pae e whakatakoto pihikete aruaru, pihikete pānui rānei. Ko te pihikete anake ko tērā e mau ai te takiuru a te kaimahi ki te wāhi whakahaere, kāore koe e kite.
