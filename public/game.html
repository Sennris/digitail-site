// A cozy-yet-unhinged adventure in the frozen wilds. // He haerenga haumaru, engari he paku pōrangi i te ngahere hukapapa.

Don't freeze.
Don't panic. Kaua e mātao.
Kaua e mataku.

The world has gone cold, but that doesn't mean you have to. Step into the boots of a reluctant hero tasked with rebuilding a fractured community. Scavenge the tundra, solve environmental puzzles, and try to keep your coffee warm while the simulation slowly falls apart around you. Kua huri te ao kia makariri, engari kāore he take kia mātao koe. Whakaekea ngā hū o tētahi toa e ngana ana ki te hanga anō i tētahi hapori kua pakaru. Rapua ngā rawa i te koraha, whakaotia ngā panga taiao, ā, me ngana kia mahana tonu tō kawhe.

[ Exploration Screenshot ] [ Pikitia Torotoro ]
Dynamic Snow & Chaotic Crafting. Hukarere Neke me te Hanga Poketere.

We spent way too long on the snow physics, so you better believe every footstep matters. Use the terrain to your advantage to trap enemies, solve physics-based puzzles, and build fires out of whatever scrap you can tape together. If it looks stupid but it works, it's not stupid. He roa rawa tō mātou wā e hanga ana i ngā ture ahupūngao hukarere, nō reira he mea nui ia tapuwae. Whakamahia te taiao hei mahanga, hei whakaoti panga, ā, hangaia he ahi mā ngā para ka kitea e koe.

[ Mechanics / Crafting Action Shot ] [ Pikitia Hanga Rawa ]
Bring them home. Whakahokia rātou ki te kāinga.

You aren't alone out there. Find lost souls wandering the wastes and bring them back to your den. Expand your base, assign tasks, and watch your ragtag group of survivors turn into a thriving, slightly dysfunctional family. Kāore koe i te mokemoke. Rapua ngā wairua kua ngaro i te koraha me te whakahoki i a rātou ki tō rua. Whakawhānuitia tō papa, tohaina ngā mahi, ā, mātakitaki i tō rōpū mōrehu e huri ana hei whānau.
