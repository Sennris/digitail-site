Cloudflare keeps only the last 30 days. Every night this site takes a copy into your own database, so this record keeps growing after Cloudflare has forgotten. Dates are UTC, not New Zealand time.

These are estimates. Cloudflare measures a sample of traffic and scales it up, and counts one person visiting ten times as ten visits. Treat them as a trend, not a headcount — especially if you are quoting a number in a funding application.
