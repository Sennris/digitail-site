We’re Digi Tails, an indie studio building worlds that look like a dream and play like a (well-balanced) fever dream. We lean into the mischief of a fox and the warmth of the flat white. We aren't interested in being the biggest studio, just the one that cares the most about its community and its code. Ko Digi Tails mātou, he whare hanga kēmu motuhake e hanga ana i ngā ao e rite ana ki te moemoeā. Ka whirinaki mātou ki te haututū o te rēpokia me te mahana o te kawhe.
