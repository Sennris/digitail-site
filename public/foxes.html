We don't just like foxes in our games; we want to protect them in the real world. If you love what we do, consider picking up a digital adoption package from the Endangered Wolf Center to support their amazing work. You even get a video of your adopted species! Kāore mātou e pai noa ki ngā rēpokia i ā mātou kēmu; e hiahia ana mātou ki te tiaki i a rātou i te ao tūturu. Mēnā he pai ki a koe tā mātou mahi, whakaarohia te koha ki te Endangered Wolf Center.
